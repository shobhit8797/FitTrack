Privacy Policy

Log Fitness (“FitTrack”, “we”, “the app”) · Last updated 5 July 2026

This policy explains what data Log Fitness collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Questions? Email shobhit@lyzr.ai.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and display name, provided when you sign in with Apple, Google, or email & password.
  • Fitness & nutrition data you log — body weight, meals, calories, macronutrients, and workouts you record in the app.
  • Meal photos — images you choose to capture or upload so the app can estimate their nutrition.
  • Apple Health data — with your permission, the app reads steps, active energy, exercise minutes, and body mass from Apple Health to show your activity and trends. This data is read on your device for display. We only store a Health value on our servers if you explicitly choose to log or send it.

How we use your information

  • To provide core features: logging, dashboards, and progress tracking.
  • To generate personalized workout and diet plans from your profile using AI.
  • To sync your data across your devices and keep you signed in.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it for tracking across other apps or websites.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

Data obtained through HealthKit is used solely to provide features inside Log Fitness. We never use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing, never sell it, and never share it with third parties for those purposes. You can revoke Health access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Health.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to operate the app. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) — hosts your account, logs, and meal photos.
  • Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In — authentication.
  • AI plan generation — the profile details and meal descriptions or photos you submit for analysis are processed by our AI provider to produce plans and nutrition estimates. This content is used only to return a result to you and is not used to advertise to you.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You are in control:

  • Export — Settings → “Export my data (JSON)” downloads a copy of your data.
  • Delete — Settings → “Delete account & all data” permanently removes your profile, logs, meal photos, and sign-in identity from our systems. This cannot be undone.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored in access-controlled Google Cloud infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children

Log Fitness is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Medical disclaimer

Log Fitness provides general fitness and nutrition information and is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before making health decisions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
